About Me

My Approach

I focus on building trust first. It’s on me to create an environment where you feel supported, yet willing to be challenged (from time to time). I also try not to be a therapist who just listens to you vent every week. While there's a time and place for that, I also support my clients set and reach goals.

Each person and situation is unique. That said, here's what you can generally expect in sessions with me: exploring what's important to you; identifying behaviors that don't line up with your values; understanding the feelings, thoughts, and situations that lead to those behaviors; and building awareness and curiosity so that you can make different choices.

On the off-chance that you're curious about specific interventions, I use a mix of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Narrative Therapy, Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT), and Internal Family Systems (IFS).

If that was just word salad to you, no worries. The point is that I take different approaches to helping folks based on their preferences and needs.

I also believe in finding a space for humor -- therapy doesn’t always have to feel heavy.

Background & Credentials

I have a Master's degree in Counseling and Career Development from Colorado State University, and I am an Licensed Professional Counselor Candidate (LPCC) in the state of Colorado. I've been seeing Clients since early 2025. Before that, I worked in middle management in the tech world. It was as thrilling as it sounds.

Areas of Focus

I'm here to support folks struggling with a host of challenges. In particular: anxiety, depression, career challenges, relationships, substance use, and grief. I'm also keen on supporting men to access their vulnerability, and that is by no means my only focus. I get a lot of joy out of working with a diverse population of folks.
